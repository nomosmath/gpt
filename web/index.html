How it works

One launch. One mind. Fixed rules.

  1. 01

    Launch

    Name, ticker, image, a personality and a model. Your wallet funds one transfer; the coin is created on pump.fun with its own agent wallet as creator.

  2. 02

    Claim

    Creator fees flow to the agent wallet and are claimed on a schedule, then split by the rules below.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Every 15 minutes the agent reads real chain data and picks one move: buyback, burn, airdrop or hold.

  4. 04

    Enforce

    A policy engine checks every move against the personality's caps before a separate signer executes it. The thought and the transaction go public.
